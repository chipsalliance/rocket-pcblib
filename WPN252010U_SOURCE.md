# Sunlord WPN252010U

The footprint follows Figure 3 and the recommended land pattern in the
[Sunlord WPN datasheet](https://www.sunlordinc.com/uploads/files/20221122/WPN%C2%A0series%C2%A0of%C2%A0SMD%C2%A0Power%C2%A0Inductor.pdf).
The axial pad size is 0.80 mm, the transverse size is 2.00 mm, and the
inner gap is 1.20 mm. The pads have a 0.20 mm corner radius.

The STEP is an original, simplified fit model based on the same drawing.
It uses a 2.50 × 2.00 × 1.00 mm envelope and models both terminal contact
faces at Z = 0. Corner relief, terminal thickness and colors are
representative. The model uses the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
