Hirose FH34SRJ-24S-0.5SH(50), LCSC C324726.

Land pattern follows Hirose FH34 catalog, January 2022, pages 3 and 5. The
24 signal copper pads are 0.30 x 0.80 mm at 0.50 mm pitch. The signal span is
11.50 mm. Two unnumbered fitting pads are 0.40 x 0.80 mm, centered at
X = +/-6.75 mm and Y = 1.40 mm. Their outside and inside spans are 13.90 and
13.10 mm. Separate signal paste openings are 0.25 x 0.65 mm, aligned with
the outer edge of the copper. The recommended stencil thickness is 0.10 mm.
The 14.0 x 3.8 mm body is 1.0 mm high. Courtyard clearance is at least
0.25 mm from the body and pads. Pin 1 is at the right of the signal row,
as shown in the manufacturer's PCB land drawing.

The connector accepts 0.30 +/-0.03 mm FPC. The standard cable drawing uses
a 2.5 +/-0.3 mm stiffener with an exposed length of 1.5 +/-0.3 mm. The
FH19SC-compatible bottom-contact variant requires at least 3.5 mm stiffener
and 2.5 +/-0.3 mm exposed length. A nominal 4 mm stiffener alone does not
prove compatibility. Camera FPC width, exposed length, thickness, pin-1
orientation and folding clearance still require supplier confirmation.

The project-generated STEP model follows the same catalog dimensions.
The closed envelope is 14.0 x 3.8 x 1.0 mm, with 24 contacts and two fittings.
Its contact plane is Z=0. Offset and rotation are zero. Scale is one.
The slot, actuator partition and internal contact shapes are simplified.
Colors identify plastic and metal. They are not manufacturer color specifications.
This dimensional model uses the repository Apache-2.0 license.

Footprint and model origins use the body center in XY, with the contact plane at Z=0.
The signal row is at footprint Y=-1.10 mm.
The origin changed by +1.10 mm in footprint Y from the original signal-row datum.
Existing boards need the same rotated origin adjustment to preserve pad positions.

Rectangular copper pads and separate paste apertures follow the manufacturer land pattern.
These dimensions take priority under KLC F4.1.
